QUESTIONS

A few things worth knowing.

How long until I notice a difference?

Many people feel a difference within the first few nights. Because your neck is adjusting to a new, more supportive position, it can take up to two weeks to feel fully natural.

What makes Lunara Sleep different from a standard pillow?

Standard pillows are a single flat shape. Lunara Sleep is contoured with distinct support zones for your neck, shoulders, and arms, so it adapts to how you actually sleep instead of asking you to adapt to it.

How many should I order?

One per sleeper is standard. Many households order a second for a guest room or for travel — and the per-pillow price drops with each one you add.

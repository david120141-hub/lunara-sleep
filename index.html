QUESTIONS

A few things worth knowing.

Isn't a soft pillow enough?

Softness provides comfort, but not necessarily alignment. A pillow that is entirely soft will deflate under the weight of your head, which can angle your cervical spine downward overnight.

Will this feel too firm?

The viscoelastic memory foam core is designed for a "slow-rebound" effect. It softens under your body warmth and pressure to cradle your neck, rather than feeling like a stiff block.

Why is it shaped like a butterfly?

The butterfly-inspired contours allow different areas of support: a central core for back sleeping, and elevated wings for side sleeping so your shoulder isn't compressed.

Can I sleep on my side?

Yes. The elevated side wings are specifically designed to fill the gap between your neck and the mattress when you sleep on your side, keeping your head level.

Can I sleep on my back?

Yes. The central cradle supports the natural curvature of your neck when you sleep on your back, preventing your chin from being pushed toward your chest.

Does the shape take time to get used to?

Many people feel a difference within the first few nights. Because your neck is adjusting to a new, more supportive position, it can take up to two weeks to feel fully natural.

Can the cover be washed?

Yes, the cooling knit cover features a zipper and is easily removable and machine-washable for simple maintenance.
